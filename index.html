Our bakery

A proper neighbourhood bakery, in the heart of Cheetham Hill

Every morning starts before sunrise at Ariana. Our bakers mix, proof and bake on site at Progress Buildings on Cheetham Hill Road — so what you take home is hours old, never days. From your daily loaf to the cake that marks your family's biggest days, everything is made here, by hand.

  • Bread and pastries baked fresh on site, every single day
  • Celebration cakes made to order — birthdays, weddings & Eid
  • Order ahead on WhatsApp and collect in store — no waiting
  • Open 7 days a week, 9 in the morning to 8 at night
